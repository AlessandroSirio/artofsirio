About me

I'm Alessandro, also known as Sirio, a concept artist and illustrator based in Italy.

I design characters, environments and visual worlds for games, books and original projects. I'm a Fine Arts Student graduating in New Art Technologies from Turin's Albertina Fine Arts Academy.

Outside client work, I build my own universes with their lore, cultures and stories: I'm deeply passionate about worldbuilding and I've been creating my own worlds and stories since I was a child.

When it comes to creating art, I approach each step with careful attention, gathering as much information as possible and doing a lot of research on the main themes of a piece of art, taking the time to explore different outcomes of an artwork.
